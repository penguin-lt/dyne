# Job 1-4, Word by Word to Strong's Numbers

*Companion to `books/job.md`. Generated 26 October 2026 from the OpenScriptures
Hebrew Bible (MorphHB), the Westminster Leningrad Codex carrying word-by-word
Strong's tags, used under its CC BY license. This is a reference file, not law
(5.1).*

**How to read it.** Each word of the Hebrew text with the Strong's number the
OSHB tags it with. The letters H plus a number are the Strong's number. A slash
inside a word divides a prefixed particle from its base, and the same notation
stands in the Strong's column:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `b/` | the prefixed preposition בְּ, in |
| `c/` | the prefixed conjunction וְ, and |
| `d/` | the definite article הַ |
| `k/` | the prefixed כְּ, like |
| `l/` | the prefixed לְ, to |
| `m/` | the prefixed מִ, from |
| `i/` | the interrogative הֲ |

Strong's numbers tag the base lexical entry. They do not carry this project's
renderings, and where 4.2 mandates a rendering against the traditional gloss,
the tables of `rulesforanacademicbible.md` govern, not Strong's.

**Verification.** All 82 verses were compared word-stream against the Sefaria
Leningrad text this volume translates. 77 of 82 matched letter for letter. The
five that differ, recorded rather than smoothed:

- **1:4** — OSHB spells their sisters with a vav (אַחְיוֹתֵיהֶם), Sefaria without (אַחְיֹתֵיהֶם)
- **1:10** — Sefaria's printed text carries an editorial pair, [אַתָּה] and (אֵת). OSHB tags אַתָּה only, and no אֵת exists in this file
- **1:21** — Sefaria spells I came out יָצָתִי without the aleph, OSHB יָצָאתִי with it
- **2:7** — Sefaria carries both readings of from...to, (עַד) and [וְעַד]. OSHB tags וְעַד only
- **3:7** — Sefaria spells let it come תָּבוֹא with the vav, OSHB תָּבָא without it
